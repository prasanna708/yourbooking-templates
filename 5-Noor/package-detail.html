The journey, day by day
DAY 1
Hyderabad to Jeddah on AIR-1001 at 08:00; Makkah by afternoon; first Umrah together after Maghrib.
DAYS 2–4
Makkah — daily prayers at the Haram, rest at your own pace.
DAY 5
Guided ziyarat: Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, Jabal al-Noor and Jabal Thawr.
DAYS 6–7
Optional second Umrah; farewell tawaf preparations.
DAY 8
The gentle road to Madinah by private coach; check-in Dar Al Eiman Grand.
DAYS 9–12
Madinah — the Rawdah with permits arranged; Quba, Qiblatain, Mount Uhud, Baqi.
DAY 13
A free day for shopping; departure briefing after Isha.
DAY 14
Home on AIR-1002; Hyderabad by 21:15.
