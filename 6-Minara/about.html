THE METHOD
Twelve years, one way of working
Fast Track started in 2014 with one group of thirty-two pilgrims from Mehdipatnam — the founder led it himself, and still leads two a year. The method has never changed: fixed dates, published prices, direct hotel contracts in both cities, and a leader walking with every forty pilgrims. The price you see is the price you pay; nothing extra appears at the airport.
