About Fast Track
It began in 2014, with thirty-two pilgrims and one promise: no family travels worried.
The founder led that first group himself and still leads two a year. Twelve years on, the method is unchanged — prices published upfront, direct hotel contracts in both cities, rooms allotted before you fly, and one leader walking with every forty pilgrims.
