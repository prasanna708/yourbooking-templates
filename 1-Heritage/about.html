About Us
HOME  /  ABOUT US
Twelve years, eight thousand pilgrims, one promise — no family travels worried.
Fast Track began in 2014 with a single group of 32 pilgrims from Mehdipatnam. The founder led that group personally — and still leads two groups every year. We are government-registered and IATA-partnered, our hotel contracts are held directly, and every group of forty travels with its own leader. What we promised that first group is still the whole business: fair prices published upfront, no hidden charges at the airport, and someone from Fast Track beside you from Hyderabad check-in to Hyderabad arrivals.
