Roles & permissions

Roles decide scope. Module grants decide screens.

Two controls work together: a person's role sets how far they can see, and per-user module grants set exactly which screens they get. Your Company Admin manages both from Settings — no call to us needed.

Role 1

Company Admin

Owns the company account — every branch, every module, every setting.

  • All modules across all branches
  • Creates users, branches and module grants
  • Masters, terms and website settings
  • Audit logs — reserved for this role
Role 2

Branch Admin

Runs one branch day to day, inside the modules granted to them.

  • Bookings, groups and money for their branch
  • Branch staff oversight
  • Reports scoped to their branch
Role 3

Staff

Sees exactly the screens their work needs — nothing else appears in the menu.

  • Only the modules granted to them
  • Booking desks see bookings; accounts staff see expenses
  • Own profile and password

Term & Conditions

Terms of using YourBooking

1. The service

YourBooking provides business software for travel companies: the back-office suite and, where subscribed, a public customer website. The service is provided to companies, for use by their authorised staff.

2. Accounts and access

Staff sign in with a mobile number and password issued under your company. You are responsible for keeping credentials safe and for what is done under your company's logins. Access levels follow the roles your company admin assigns.

3. Subscription and renewal

The service is licensed per company for an activation period. When the period ends without renewal, access may be suspended until renewal is completed. SMS and WhatsApp message packs are prepaid counts consumed as messages are sent.

4. Your data

Bookings, passenger records, rates and website content entered by your company remain your company's data. We process it to operate the service and do not sell it.

5. Passenger information

Your company is responsible for collecting passenger details — including passport and identity information — lawfully and with the passenger's knowledge, and for their accuracy.

6. Availability and support

We aim to keep the service available and to resolve issues promptly during working hours. Planned maintenance is notified in advance where possible.

7. Acceptable use

  • No attempts to access another company's data
  • No use of messaging packs for content unrelated to your travel business
  • No reselling of the service without a written agreement

8. Liability

The service supports your business decisions but does not replace them; fares, visas and travel arrangements remain between your company, your customers and your suppliers.

9. Changes

We may update these terms; meaningful changes are notified to company admins before they take effect.

Privacy Policy

What we store, and why

1. Information we hold

  • Company and staff details — names, mobile numbers, email addresses and role assignments used for sign-in and administration.
  • Passenger records — details your company enters to run bookings, which can include passport information and identity numbers where your processes require them.
  • Business records — bookings, advances, expenses, invoices and website content belonging to your company.

2. Why we process it

To operate the suite: creating bookings and invoices, calculating balances, publishing your website where subscribed, and sending booking notifications by SMS, WhatsApp and email.

3. Sharing

Booking details are shared only as needed to fulfil travel arrangements — for example with airlines, hotels and transport providers — and with the messaging and email providers that deliver notifications. We do not sell data.

4. Storage and retention

Data is stored securely and retained while your company subscription is active, and thereafter as required for legal and accounting purposes.

5. Your choices

Company admins control staff access and can correct or remove records. Passengers can approach the travel company that holds their booking to correct their details.

6. Cookies

The applications use session cookies for sign-in. The public websites use only what is needed for the site to function.

7. Contact

Privacy questions reach us through the Contact page.
