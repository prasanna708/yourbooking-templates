JOURNEY 01 · FOURTEEN DAYS
Economy Umrah
Fourteen days, two cities, one purpose — and every practical thing already thought of before you ask.
7 NIGHTS MAKKAH5 NIGHTS MADINAHGROUP OF FORTYDEPARTS HYDERABAD
THE SHAPE OF THE JOURNEY
DAY 1
Hyderabad to Jeddah on the morning flight; Makkah by afternoon; first Umrah together after Maghrib.
DAYS 2–7
Makkah. The Haram a short walk away; a guided ziyarat day to Mina, Arafat and Jabal al-Noor.
DAY 8
The road to Madinah, unhurried, by private coach.
DAYS 9–13
Madinah. The Rawdah with permits arranged; Quba, Qiblatain, Uhud; evenings left deliberately free.
DAY 14
The farewell, and home by night.
WHERE YOU STAY
A few minutes’ walk, in both cities.
Al Kiswah Towers in Makkah, four hundred and fifty metres from the Haram; Dar Al Eiman Grand in Madinah, three hundred metres from Masjid an-Nabawi. Breakfast and dinner are laid each day; rooms are allotted before you fly.
